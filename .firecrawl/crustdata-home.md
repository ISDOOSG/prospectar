## Delivered in bulk. Refreshed monthly.\
\
```\
SELECT\
   c.company_id,\
   c.company_website,\
   c.total_investment_usd,\
   c.linkedin_profile_url,\
   g.headcount,\
   g.headcount_qoq_pct,\
   c.days_since_last_fundraise,\
   t.monthly_visitor_qoq_pct\
FROM\
   crustdata_companies c\
JOIN\
   companies_growth g ON c.company_id = g.company_id\
JOIN\
   companies_monthly_web_traffic_growth t ON c.company_id = t.company_id\
WHERE\
   g.headcount > 50\
   AND g.headcount_qoq_pct > 20\
   AND c.days_since_last_fundraise >= 730\
   AND c.total_funding_raised_usd > 5000000\
   AND c.largest_headcount_country = "USA";\
   AND t.monthly_visitor_qoq_pct > 5\
```\
